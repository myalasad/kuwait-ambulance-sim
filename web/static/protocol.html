AGreen corridor for ambulancesdetection → enable → restore

Intent: an ambulance with lights on never waits at a red light on its route, and no signal is ever switched unsafely.

A1Camera detection and confirmation

Trigger
An ambulance with active emergency lights enters a junction camera's range along one of its approaches.
Action
The detection is reported; the first detection confirms the ambulance to the control centre, which already holds its planned route.
Ends when
The mission ends or the lights are switched off.
Logged as
cameracorridor operations on the ambulance's A-case.
Parameters
camera_range_m = 200

A2Enabling a junction (purposely enabled)

Trigger
A confirmed ambulance's ETA to a signal on its route drops below the lead time — never later than the minimum distance, and never earlier than the maximum except under rule A7 (queue flush), which may double the lead when the approach ahead is congested.
Action
Amber to conflicting traffic → all-red clearance → the ambulance's approach is held on protected green while every cross approach shows the flashing-amber yield state of rule A6. Cars in front of the ambulance discharge through the green. Signals are never switched dark.
Ends when
Rule A3 (pass) or A4 (hold limit).
Logged as
P-case opens: preempt_startpreempt_phase (amber, all-red, corridor active). Map label PURPOSELY ENABLED.
Parameters
greenwave_lead_s = 25 greenwave_min_m = 160 greenwave_distance_m = 800 yellow_time_s = 3 allred_time_s = 2

A3Restoring after the ambulance passes

Trigger
The ambulance has passed the junction and is physically out of the junction box (see G4).
Action
Corridor held for a short clearance, then amber, then the normal programme resumes.
Ends when
Immediately re-armed if another ambulance is inbound (B4).
Logged as
restore closes the P-case with the hold duration. Map label BACK TO NORMAL.
Parameters
clearance_after_pass_s = 2 yellow_time_s = 3

A4Hold limit — cross traffic is never starved

Trigger
A junction has been held continuously for the maximum time while the ambulance is still beyond camera range.
Action
Release the corridor and guarantee cross traffic a normal cycle (cooldown) before re-arming.
Ends when
Cooldown elapses, or the ambulance reaches camera range (it is then served regardless).
Logged as
hold_limit then restore on the P-case.
Parameters
max_hold_s = 90 preempt_cooldown_s = 20

A5Ambulance physically inside the junction

Trigger
The ambulance is on the junction's internal lanes.
Action
The hold is kept even if the signal is otherwise due to release — cross traffic must not receive green into an occupied box.
Ends when
The ambulance exits the box.
Logged as
Covered by the open P-case.
Parameters
—

A6Flashing amber for cross traffic, hardening to red

Trigger
A junction is enabled for an ambulance (A2) and the unit is still on its way in.
Action
A junction under emergency control blinks for the whole hold — unmistakable to every driver. Far out, cross approaches show a flashing amber ("signal overridden — yield"): drivers may cross carefully when clear, and vehicles caught inside the box can drain out instead of being sealed in by a hard red. The flash hardens to flashing red (absolute stop — the physical signal state is red) when the unit is close in time — clearance ahead of an ambulance is a time quantity: at speed it hardens ~180 m out, in crawling traffic the amber flash persists until the unit is genuinely near — and always within the absolute minimum distance. If the unit falls back (e.g. re-arbitration), the amber flash resumes with hysteresis so the junction never flickers between the two.
Ends when
The corridor is released (A3/A4) and the normal programme resumes.
Logged as
preempt_phase on the P-case: "cross approaches to FLASHING AMBER (yield)" then "cross flash hardened to RED for final clearance" with the live distance and time-to-junction.
Parameters
flash_amber = true flash_harden_eta_s = 12 flash_harden_min_m = 60

A7Queue flush — the corridor looks after signals ahead

Trigger
A signal ahead on the confirmed route whose approach is CONGESTED or JAMMED (live Markov state) comes within twice the normal activation lead.
Action
The junction is enabled early so the standing queue drains through the green before the ambulance arrives — an ambulance with a predetermined route clears numerous signals ahead, not just the next one. Once flush-enabled the request is held for that unit (no flapping if the congestion state flickers). All A4/A6 protections still apply: the hold cap, cooldown, and the flashing-amber/red sequence.
Ends when
The unit passes (A3), or the hold cap releases the junction (A4).
Logged as
The P-case's preempt_start carries the justification: "enabled EARLY (queue flush): this approach is congested, so the extra lead drains the standing queue before the unit arrives".
Parameters
flush_lead_factor = 2 greenwave_distance_m = 800 (absolute cap)

BPriority conflictstwo ambulances, one junction

Intent: deterministic, auditable rulings — and a human decision whenever the machine criteria cannot separate the candidates.

B1Continuity

Trigger
A junction already serving one corridor receives a request from a second ambulance.
Action
Keep serving the current ambulance; the second queues. Switching mid-approach would trap both streams.
Ends when
The first ambulance passes; the second is served next.
Logged as
arbitration
Parameters
—

B2Clear margin — nearest wins

Trigger
Two fresh requests; one ambulance is nearer by more than the tie margin.
Action
Grant the nearest; queue the other.
Ends when
The granted ambulance passes.
Logged as
arbitration naming both vehicles and distances.
Parameters
arbitration_tie_m = 20

B3Tie — unable to decide → operator referral

Trigger
Two fresh requests within the tie margin of each other.
Action
The junction stays on its normal programme (the safe state); a D-case opens; the conflict is pushed to the operator on the Live Map and Operations page with one-click grant buttons.
Ends when
A supervisor grants, the conflict dissolves, or the timeout applies the default policy (nearest).
Logged as
D-case: decision_referred decision_made decision_moot
Parameters
arbitration_tie_m = 20 operator_timeout_s = 8

B4Same approach — shared corridor

Trigger
Two ambulances on the same approach.
Action
Both use the same green; the follower re-arms the junction during its clearing window.
Ends when
The follower passes.
Logged as
Same P-case, re-armed.
Parameters
clearance_after_pass_s = 2

CAmbulance mission lifecyclenothing leaves the map silently

Intent: every ambulance has an A-case from dispatch to close, and the close reason is always on record.

C1Dispatch — nearest AVAILABLE unit, always from a hospital

Trigger
An operator dispatches to an incident scene (map click or named area).
Action
Origin is a hospital under the ready-fleet model: each hospital stations a limited number of ready units; a dispatch commits one, and a crew that delivers a patient rejoins the receiving hospital's pool only after turnaround. "Auto" ranks every hospital by Dijkstra travel time to the scene in a single pass, keeps only hospitals with a ready unit, and among those within the rotation tolerance of the fastest picks the one with the fewest crews already out — real EMS coverage, and never a convoy from a single gate. If every crew is committed, the call queues and the next crew to finish turnaround responds automatically (logged with queue position and wait); a gate-headway rule keeps two departures from stacking at one gate. Route computed (E1), speed exemption armed (F1), lights on.
Ends when
The unit's crew completes turnaround and returns to a ready pool (logged: "unit READY again at X (n/2)").
Logged as
A-case opens: dispatch with route length, signals on route, ETA, routing basis — and, when not the nearest gate, the full ruling ("X is nearest but has no ready units (0/2 — crews on mission or in turnaround); Y responds in T s with 1/2 ready"). Live ready counts are shown at every hospital marker on the map.
Parameters
snap_radius_m per scenario hospital_ready_units = 2 unit_turnaround_s = 180 dispatch_rotation_tolerance = 0.25

C3Hot return — reroute to the nearest hospital

Trigger

Ends when

Parameters

C8Stuck in traffic — adaptive reroute

Trigger
An en-route ambulance advances less than the progress threshold within the stuck window (stop-and-go creep counts as stuck).
Action
Re-plan from the current position under live weights. A meaningfully faster corridor (≥10% + 5 s) is applied immediately — the signal corridor and driver navigation follow. If the alternatives are equally congested, the ambulance holds course and says so: rerouting for its own sake is motion without progress. Rescue lanes (sublane model) are active on both network models so ambulances filter through queues physically.
Ends when
Progress resumes; re-checked each cooldown.
Logged as
reroute — either "ADAPTIVE REROUTE around the blockage via …" or "checked for a faster corridor: none exists".
Parameters
stuck_progress_m = 40 stuck_after_s = 25 stuck_reroute_cooldown_s = 60

DOrdinary traffic — early greendemand-responsive signals

Intent: nobody waits at a red light on an empty crossing; everyone gets the fair timer the moment anyone else is waiting.

D1Early green for the only occupied approach

Trigger
Exactly one approach occupied; every other approach empty for the confirmation window and carrying no traffic at all within the quiet window; no ambulance involved.
Action
Through the junction's own amber, move to the phase serving that approach and hold it (or extend it if already green).
Ends when
Rule D2.
Logged as
actuation. Map label EARLY GREEN.
Parameters
lone_confirm_s = 3 lone_quiet_s = 20 lone_min_green_s = 5

D2Fairness returns instantly

Trigger
Any other approach becomes occupied, the lone traffic has passed, or the cap is reached.
Action
End the early green (after the minimum green) and resume the normal fixed-time cycle — with several approaches occupied everyone takes turns.
Ends when
—
Logged as
actuation with the reason.
Parameters
lone_max_hold_s = 30

D3Cooldown and precedence

Trigger
An early green has just ended; or an ambulance corridor arrives at the junction.
Action
No new early green for the cooldown; ambulance preemption always outranks early green and takes the junction immediately.
Ends when
Cooldown elapses.
Logged as
—
Parameters
actuation_cooldown_s = 10

D4Saturation — fair timers by design

Trigger
Heavy traffic: several approaches of a junction are occupied at once — the normal condition on Kuwaiti weekdays 13:00–21:00, on weekends 13:00–24:00, and throughout the Extreme traffic level.
Action
The early-green rule does not apply; the junction runs its normal fixed-time cycle so every approach gets its fair turn. This is not a failure of the priority system — it is the rule working as intended, and the system counts it.
Ends when
Traffic thins to a single occupied approach (D1 becomes possible again).
Logged as
A five-minute actuation traffic check ("N of M occupied junctions have several approaches occupied — fair timers by design"); the Live Map shows the live share of busy junctions on fair timers.
Parameters
traffic_level day_type start_hour

ERouting & predictionone route object, two consumers

Intent: the driver's navigation and the signal corridor are the same route, and the route anticipates congestion rather than reacting to it.

E1Dijkstra routing

Trigger
Dispatch (C1) and hot return (C3).
Action
Shortest-time route over the edge graph — one-way streets and turn restrictions respected — assigned to the vehicle and read by the control centre to know which junction comes next and when.
Ends when
—
Logged as
Route length, signals and ETA on the dispatch operation; node-by-node table on the Navigation page.
Parameters
route_live_weights = True

E2Predictive weights (Markov chains)

Trigger
Routing, when a corridor has enough observed history.
Action
Each road is weighed by its CTMC-forecast speed at the moment the ambulance will reach it (time-dependent Dijkstra), so routes avoid where congestion will be. The DTMC supplies long-run congestion shares for analytics. Corridors with too little history fall back to pooled road-class chains.
Ends when
—
Logged as
The dispatch operation states whether predictive routing changed the corridor versus live-only routing, and by how much.
Parameters
markov_routing = True markov_sample_s = 30 markov_min_obs = 40 markov_max_edges = 160

E3Prediction is validated, not assumed

Trigger
Every observation period, for every corridor with history.
Action
A 5-minute forecast is filed and later scored against what actually happened, alongside two naive baselines (persistence: "stays as now"; climatology: "long-run average"). The Copilot page reports hit rate, Brier score and the Brier skill score against each baseline — zero or negative skill is shown as such.
Ends when
Continuous; scores persist across sessions.
Logged as
Copilot page: forecast accuracy card, per-corridor matrices and recent forecasts with outcomes.
Parameters
horizon 300 s

IData, models & scopewhat is real

I1Provenance

Real
Road networks, one-way system, turn restrictions, signal locations and street names (OpenStreetMap); hospital locations.
Calibrated
Background demand follows the published shape of Kuwaiti weekly traffic, with the volume anchored to Kuwait's ~2.4 million registered vehicles (PACI) and an operator estimate for the downtown core (a weekend evening at Extreme ≈ 3,000+ vehicles on the core network at once). Kuwait exposes no public live feed and Google Maps publishes travel times, not vehicle counts. Real hourly counts (e.g. the licensed xMap Kuwait catalog) drop into data/real_counts.csv as hour,multiplier and override the profile.
Synthetic
Individual signal timing plans (static 72–90 s cycles per junction); replaceable with real MOI plans in the network file.
Learned
Markov chains learn from simulated traffic here; the identical estimator ingests real detector data unchanged.

I2Network models

Downtown
Kuwait City core, every street, sublane model with rescue-lane behaviour.
Metro
All six governorates at arterial level (motorways, trunks, primary and secondary roads; residential side streets not modelled), a real MoH hospital per governorate, ~100 named areas.

I3Calendar and traffic scenarios

Day type
Weekday (Sun–Thu): 06:30–08:30 peak, congested 13:00–21:00, near-empty 01:00–05:00. Weekend (Fri–Sat): quiet 01:00–12:00, congested 13:00–24:00.
Traffic level
Easy ×0.45, Medium ×1.0 (calibrated baseline), Extreme ×1.8 — on top of the hourly shape. One flat peak-rate demand base is scaled live each hour; any start hour 0–23 is selectable on the Live Map.
Parameters
day_type traffic_level start_hour demand_hours = 3

Action

Scope of use

This is a planning, evaluation and training simulator. It is not a certified life-safety control system and must not be connected to live signal hardware without the engineering, redundancy and regulatory review that real emergency-vehicle-preemption deployments require. Decisions about real dispatch or real signal control must not be made on simulation output alone.
