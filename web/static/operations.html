Arrival-time comparison — with vs without signal preemption

Method & weights. The WITH bar is the measured run: free-flow time at the exempt speed profile, plus measured traffic delay, plus any residual signal wait. The WITHOUT bar adds the expected red-light wait at every signal on the route from that junction's real programme: a vehicle arriving at a random point of the cycle waits E[w] = r² / 2C (r = red time for the approach, C = cycle). Because this term is quadratic in the signal timer, the timer is the highest-weight variable in the comparison — traffic enters linearly through the measured delay, and the no-traffic bounds isolate the timer's effect completely.

Decision-Making Matrix (DMM) — how this software rules

Every ruling the controller makes comes from this matrix. Criteria are weighted lexicographically — a higher row always beats every row below it, so the outcome is deterministic, auditable, and never a blend: SAFETY > CONTINUITY > SIGNAL-TIMER / PROXIMITY > FAIRNESS > HUMAN REFERRAL when machine criteria cannot separate the candidates. Each applied rule is logged with a case id.
