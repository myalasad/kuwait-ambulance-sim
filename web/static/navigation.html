Route

The driver's navigation and the signal corridor are the same route object: computed once with Dijkstra over the road network's edge graph (one-way streets and turn restrictions respected), weighted by live travel times, then assigned to the vehicle and used by the control centre to know which junction the ambulance reaches next.
