Ask the record — answers grounded in cases, protocol and live analytics, with citations

Ask me anything the system has recorded: why a junction was enabled, what an ambulance did, who decided a conflict, which corridors are likely to jam. Every answer cites its sources [in brackets] — and if the record doesn't contain the answer, I say so instead of guessing.
Cost design: answers come from Haiku 4.5 (fractions of a cent), escalating to Sonnet 5 only for reports or when grounding needs a second pass. Retrieval is hybrid — exact-entity filters + BM25 — free and local. With no API key the copilot still answers in extractive mode. It is read-only: no path to the signal controls.

Each corridor is a 4-state chain (FREE/SLOW/CONGESTED/JAMMED). A DTMC gives the long-run (stationary) congested share; a CTMC generator gives P(state at any horizon) — that forecast feeds the router, which weighs each road by its predicted speed at the moment the ambulance will reach it, not just its speed now. The chains learn from the running simulation and persist across sessions — the model feeds itself constantly.
